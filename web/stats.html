How it works

Every morning a job runs on GitHub Actions: it collects posts from public job APIs, merges duplicates, has an AI model tag each new post, and publishes this site. These are the numbers from the latest run.

The daily pipeline

Five steps, about a minute of work plus AI tagging for the posts that are new that day.

Duplicates removed

The same job is often listed on several sites or posted twice. Exact link matches go first, then near-identical titles at the same company and city.

Where posts come from

Only official APIs and public career-board feeds, used within each site's terms.
